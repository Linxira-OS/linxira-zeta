Zeta · Identity exploration · 2026

A flatter, rounder Zeta.

Six directions that keep the LZ monogram and the Zeta name. Built on a 64-unit grid with rounded caps, round joins, flat fills, and one coral accent at most. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D. The starting point is assets/zeta-mark.svg — a geometric L + Z in teal and coral with square caps; these concepts evolve that idea without touching production.

01

Keep the LZ read

Every direction still spells L and Z. The hierarchy is Z first, L second — the name leads, the monogram holds it.

02

One continuous idea

Prefer one ribbon, one tile, or one loop over assembled parts. Shared rails are better than seams.

03

Scale down honestly

If a detail disappears at 16px, the concept needs a declared small-size fallback — not a blurry compromise.

04

Mono first

Teal is the default, coral is optional. The mark must survive one ink, one paper, and a transparent cutout.

Six concepts

Big preview, real sizes, construction.

Construction. 64 grid, 7.5u stroke, round caps and joins. Key points: (14,14) (50,14) (50,50) (14,50); the diagonal lands at (28,50). The L foot and the Z bottom rail are one shared rail. Safe area 8u; optical center 32,32.

RationaleThe L’s stem and foot become the Z’s bottom rail. One path, no seams: the eye reads Z first, then finds the L holding its left edge. It is the closest evolution of the current mark, with the square caps and miter joins replaced by rounded strokes.

App iconThe mark sits at about 68% of the 512 tile, with a 10% clear margin. Teal on the dark tile; ink on a light tile.

Watch-outDo not shorten the L foot; the bottom-left rail is what makes the L read. Do not add a second stroke to “fix” the diagonal.

s = widened 12u aperture for 16px; the standard 9u cut is used at 32 and 64.

Construction. Tile 8–56, r12. Aperture 9u, round caps: (15,18) → (40,18), diagonal to (24,46), exit at (60,46). The cut is transparent (mask / evenodd), not a painted line. Safe area 8u; the tile is the mark.

One color; the cut stays transparent. Never fill the aperture with a second color.

RationaleA rounded tile with a Z-shaped aperture. The solid left and bottom edges read as the L; the cut reads as the Z. The mark is one flat shape, so the background becomes the second color — no outline, no gradient, no shadow.

FaviconAt 16 the 9u cut closes into a blob; the declared 16px asset widens the aperture to 12u in a 4–60 crop. At 32 and 64 the standard cut is used.

App iconThe tile sits at about 75% of the 512 icon on a dark background; the aperture shows the dark app tile. Keep the outer corner radius consistent with the platform mask.

Watch-outDo not use the standard cut at 16px. Do not add an inner border to separate the tile from the background; the negative space is the point.

Recommendation

Lead with Fold. Extend with Cluster.

Fold is the safest evolution of the current mark: one geometry, one ink, no accent dependency, and a clean 16px read. Cluster is the strongest storytelling alternate for Team and multi-agent surfaces, provided the 16px collapse ships with it.

01 · Fold

Primary

One continuous ribbon: L stem and foot become the Z bottom rail. Flat, rounded, and native to the dark teal system. Coral is optional, so the mark can live anywhere.

  • Scalability: best in the set. A single 7.5u stroke, no counters, no accent; 16px is a clean Z with an L edge.
  • Recognizability: reads Z in the first glance; the L is a second read, which is the right hierarchy for the Zeta name.
  • Brand fit: direct evolution of the geometric LZ, now flat and round; dark-first and teal-native.
  • Use: primary product mark, favicon, app icon, docs, terminal chrome, loading states.

04 · Cluster

Alternate

Two rounded tiles with L and Z. The transparent gap keeps the cluster readable in one color; the coral tile is a controlled second-agent accent.

  • Scalability: strong at 32 and 64; needs the declared single-tile 16px fallback. The extra asset is worth it when the team story matters.
  • Recognizability: L and Z are explicit; the cluster reads Team / multi-agent immediately.
  • Brand fit: good for collaboration and agent surfaces; coral is limited to the second tile.
  • Use: team and multi-agent launches, collaborative surfaces, secondary brand moments. Not the only mark in 16px contexts.

Do not use

The shortcuts that break this system:

  • Aperture’s standard 9u cut at 16px — use the 12u small cut.
  • Cluster at 16px without the single-tile fallback.
  • Loop below 32px; the loop closes into a blob.
  • Prompt’s coral dot as a status light, badge, or the only differentiator.
  • Gradients, shadows, bevels, 3D, square caps, or strokes below 7u.
  • Coral as a large background; keep it at or below 10% of the mark area.
  • A non-rounded wordmark, or text inside the mark.
  • Rotated tiles, perspective, or off-grid optical “fixes” that break 16px.

System rules

One grid, one accent, no tricks.

These rules apply to every concept. The mark is flat by construction: if a direction needs a shadow to read, it is not ready.

Geometry

  • 64-unit master grid; export from the grid, never trace a PNG.
  • 8u safe area inside the 64 tile; app icon keeps a 10% clear margin.
  • 7.5u stroke for primary marks; 5–6u only inside tiles. Round caps and round joins.
  • Radii 8–14u; optical center at 32,32; nudge ±1u only when a loop or diagonal makes the mass uneven.

Color

  • Teal #2AC9C1 is the primary mark color on dark.
  • Coral #F36F5D is an accent: one dot, one tile, never a large field; ≤10% of the mark area.
  • Ink #0E1417 on paper; Paper #F2F5F4 on ink.
  • No gradients, no opacity fades, no tinted shadows. Transparent cuts only.

Clear space & sizes

  • Clear space: 0.5× mark height on all sides.
  • Favicon: 16 / 32 / 64; use the declared 16px fallback where needed.
  • App icon: 512 export, mark at 60–75%, 10% margin.
  • Wordmark lockup: mark 1.25× wordmark, gap 0.30×, baseline aligned.

Exports

  • SVG with strokes editable; outline for production if the platform requires it.
  • PNG 512 and 1024 with transparent background; dark and light variants.
  • ICO 16 / 32 / 48 / 64; maskable 512 with 10% safe padding.
  • Name by concept and size: zeta-fold-512.png, zeta-cluster-64.png.

Do

  • Keep the Z read first and the L second.
  • Use the transparent cut as the second color.
  • Ship the declared small-size fallback with the main asset.
  • Test on ink, paper, and a mid-gray browser chrome.
  • Align the lockup to the mark’s visual bottom, not its bounding box.

Don’t

  • Add a gradient, shadow, bevel, or 3D perspective.
  • Use square caps, miter joins, or hairline strokes.
  • Paint the negative cut with a second color.
  • Use coral as a background or as a large tile fill.
  • Set the wordmark in a non-rounded font, or put text inside the mark.
